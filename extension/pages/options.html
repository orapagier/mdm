My Download Manager

Settings apply immediately.

Capture

Images are only taken when they are actually being downloaded — saved, or fetched through a link that asks for a download. One opened in a tab is still shown, not saved.

Chat and gallery sites often assemble a file in the page and hand the browser a blob: link to it, which no downloader can fetch. MDM asks the page for those bytes instead. Turn this off to leave them to the browser.

Exclusions

MDM captures every download regardless of its size or type, so that the list and the category folders hold all of them rather than the large ones. These two lists are how you carve out the exceptions.
